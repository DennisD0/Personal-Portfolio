01 — About

Still learning, still building.

Dennis — Class of 2026

“I like working on real products that solve real problems.”

When I was young I always loved building. I loved Legos, and I would try to take whatever I was imagining and actually make it exist through some kind of medium. As I grew up that medium changed, from Legos to drawings to content creation and now software. The interest stayed the same though.

I got to work in software a few different ways, first as a software engineering intern at the Research Foundation of CUNY, then as a project manager at NASA L’SPACE, and then as a product designer at Scout. Each one taught me different tools, from Figma and Miro for design and discovery to project tools for keeping a team on track.

The more comfortable I got with those tools, the more I noticed how much of the work around them could be automated, and that is what pulled me into AI workflows. So I jumped deep into it and started MavenStudio, where I work with small businesses and build the thing they actually need. Along the way I realized I have always liked building things that had a purpose and solved some kind of problem, and that is what product managers do. So that is the work I am looking for now.
