01 — About

From ambiguous problems to shipped products.

“I care most about the handoff — the moment a user’s problem becomes a structured spec, and the spec becomes a working interface.”

I’m a Computer Science student at CUNY Queens College, but most of my hands-on experience sits at the intersection of product and engineering. As a Product Designer at Scout, I shipped 20+ high-fidelity Figma flows across onboarding, discovery, and profile experiences while supporting early-stage product discovery. Before that, I led an 8-member, 5-subsystem team at NASA L’SPACE through a full Agile program review cycle, and earlier worked as a Software Engineer Intern at the Research Foundation of CUNY. Outside of work, I build fast — my hackathon projects EmpathiRead and GreenIQ were designed, built, and pitched in 24–36 hour sprints.
